# Content
Topic: Mental Health Apps

## Overview/Introduction/Context 
* Mental health apps are apps that help people care for their minds.
* They can help people relax and manage stress.
* People can use them on phones, tablets, and computers.
* Some apps have videos, games, music, and exercises.
* They can help people learn healthy ways to manage emotions.

## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* Headspace
  * It is an app for meditation and relaxation.
  * It has guided meditation videos and audio.
  * It teaches people how to relax.
  * It has exercises for stress and sleep.
  * People can choose short or long activities.
  * It works on iPhones and Android phones.
  * It can also be used on a computer.
  * Some sessions can be downloaded to use offline.
  * [Here are the website](https://www.headspace.com)

##### Hardware
* Smartphone / Tablet
  * Examples: iPhone, iPad, and Samsung.
  * They allow people to download mental health apps.
  * People can listen to relaxing music.
  * They can watch videos and follow exercises.
  * They have speakers for guided meditation.
  * They use Wi-Fi or mobile data to connect to apps.
  * They have touchscreens that make apps easy to use.
    

## Part B: possible future technology
* AI Mental Health Assistant
  * An app that uses artificial intelligence (AI).
  * It could give people personalized relaxation activities.
  * It could suggest exercises based on their feelings.
  * It could help people track their moods.
  * It could notice changes in sleep habits.
  * It could give reminders to take breaks.
  * It could help people find professional support.
  * It could work with smartwatches.
  * It could make mental health support easier to access.
  * It would need strong privacy and safety rules.
  * It would not replace doctors or therapists.
